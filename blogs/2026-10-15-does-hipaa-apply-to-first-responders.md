Yes, HIPAA will apply to a first responder if their agency is a covered entity. HIPAA generally applies to EMS agencies that provide healthcare and transmit health information electronically in connection with transactions covered by HIPAA , such as submitting electronic insurance claims. It is often the case for ambulance or EMS companies.
